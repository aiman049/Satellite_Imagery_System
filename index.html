Future Aspects

This project focuses on creating a satellite imagery database system capable of storing geo-spatial images and observational data for different countries. On much advanced level the satellite imagery data by 'WAN Satellite Imagery' can be used for training simple GIS based AI models, Urban Planning, Agriculture and Climate Observation, Disaster Management and for creating 3-D Models.
